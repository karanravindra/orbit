# User guide

How to use the Orbit web app.

## Create an account

1. Open **Sign up** and enter your details. Passwords found in known data
   breaches are rejected.
2. Orbit emails you a verification code. Enter it within 5 minutes. You are
   signed in once it is accepted.
3. Lost the code? Use **Resend code**.

**Forgot your password?** Choose **Forgot password**, enter the code from your
email, and set a new one. This signs you out of all devices.

## Your workspace

Every account has a personal workspace, created automatically. You are its
owner. You can't delete it, leave it, or transfer ownership. You can't create
other workspaces, but you can be invited to them. Switch workspaces from the
user menu.

## Invite people

In **Settings → Invitations**, send an invitation by email and choose a role.
Only workspace owners and admins can invite. Invitations expire after 48 hours;
you can resend or cancel them. Invitees follow the link, sign in or sign up,
and accept or decline.

## Members and roles

**Settings → Members** lists everyone in the workspace. Members can hold more
than one role. Roles are `owner`, `admin`, `member`, or a custom role.

**Settings → Roles** lets admins create custom roles by choosing exactly which
actions they allow (for example, managing teams but not members). Only owners
and admins can assign the admin role, whatever a custom role allows.

## Teams

**Settings → Teams** lets you create, rename, and delete teams, add or remove
members, and pick your active team. What you see depends on your permissions.

## Your account

**Account** has your profile, password change, and active sessions, which you
can revoke individually.

## Platform admins

If your email is on the operator's admin allowlist, a **Platform admin** entry
appears in the user menu. From there you can create and edit users, set roles,
ban, set passwords, revoke sessions, delete users, and impersonate a user for
one hour. A banner lets you return to your own account.
